FReader HWP/HWPX reader

The FReader host files (index.html, reader.css and reader.js) adapt the original
FReader reading UI to the unmodified rhwp document engine. Documents are opened
read-only. No document is uploaded for conversion or rendering.

included. The original document is never rewritten.
