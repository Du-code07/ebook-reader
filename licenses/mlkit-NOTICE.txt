Google ML Kit Text Recognition (com.google.mlkit:text-recognition-korean:16.0.1)
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit

ML Kit is not open source software. It is provided by Google under the ML Kit
Terms of Service (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and the Google APIs
Terms of Service. FReader uses it on the device to recognize text in scanned
documents. Scanned images and recognized text are processed on the device; the
SDK may send diagnostic and usage information to Google, as described in
Google's ML Kit documentation.
